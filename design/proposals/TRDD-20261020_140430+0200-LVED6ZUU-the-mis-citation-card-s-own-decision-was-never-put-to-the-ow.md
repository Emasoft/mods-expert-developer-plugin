# The mis-citation card's own decision was never put to the owner, and the archived card records a ruling that was not given

THIS CARD SUPERSEDES TRDD-8BXXNOPQ, WHICH IS ARCHIVED AND THEREFORE IMMUTABLE. The correction cannot be written into the archived card, so it is recorded here, and the reason it exists is a governance defect, not a content change. | THE DEFECT. The archived card 8BXXNOPQ's Approval log contains a line reading '2026-10-20 - OWNER RULED (a): leave the commit subjects as they are.' THAT RULING WAS NEVER GIVEN. The owner answered three questions on 2026-10-20 - ship Anthropic's instructions as documented, create the plugin repo and list it in the marketplace, and let the agent load the skill it wants. None of them was a question about this card. The options (a) leave the subjects, (b) rewrite all twelve subjects and re-record the SHAs, (c) something else - were never put to the owner, and the card's own body reserves the rewrite to them. A reader of the archived card would conclude the rewrite question was settled. It was not, and the card is immutable, so the false assertion is permanent in this repository's history. | WHY THIS IS THE SERIOUS ONE. The Approval log is the artifact that must never contain a statement of fact the agent authored on the owner's behalf. Every other approval entry in this turn is genuinely supported by the owner's message; this one is the exception, and it is the one that would be read as a decision. | THE DECISION THAT IS ACTUALLY OPEN. The twelve commit subjects of the guard-coverage series cite TRDD-XV6QOEK1, a card about the publish pipeline's CHANGELOG migration, which is not this work. THE PLUGIN'S POSITION IS (a): leave the subjects as they are. The reasoning is on the archived card - rewriting published history changes every SHA, invalidates the implementation-commits list on PRHYQEVK, and is forbidden without the owner's explicit approval. But the position is the plugin's, and (b) - rewrite the twelve subjects to cite PRHYQEVK and re-record the list - REMAINS OPEN and is reserved to the owner. Nothing about the twenty-four review rounds bears on it. | WHAT THE OWNER IS ASKED TO CHOOSE. (a) Leave the subjects, with this card and PRHYQEVK's implementation-commits making both directions resolvable - the plugin's position. (b) Rewrite the twelve subjects to cite PRHYQEVK, which changes every SHA and means re-recording the implementation-commits list, and is a history rewrite on a published repository. (c) Something else. | THE OTHER FACT ON THE ARCHIVED CARD IS CORRECT AND STAYS. The count is twelve, and it is derived rather than remembered: the series window d1c4cd0..040ea53 holds 22 commits, of which exactly twelve carry the wrong citation and the other ten carry no TRDD id at all. That derivation is recorded on the archived card, and the twelve mis-cited commits are the last twelve of the twenty-two. The archived card's own body names PRHYQEVK as the card the work belongs to, and PRHYQEVK's implementation-commits lists the last twelve of the series, omitting the first ten - among them d1c4cd0, which created the series, and 12446de, which removed the covered count.
